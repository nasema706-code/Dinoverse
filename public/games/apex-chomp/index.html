Collect every golden orb to clear a world. You have three lives. Keep away from the spirits — until you find a Super bite orb.

P or Escape to pause.
On your phoneSwipe anywhere on the maze,
or tap the direction buttons.

Tap your next direction a little early; your dinosaur will turn at the next opening. You can reverse at any time.
